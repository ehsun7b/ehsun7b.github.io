Career Highlights

Here are some of the significant projects I've worked on throughout my career.

Atlassian (Australia)

At Atlassian, I worked in the Platform team on a significant project aimed at redesigning and centralizing the authentication and authorization processes across all of the company's projects. This involved building a unified, secure system that ensured seamless user management and access control across Atlassian's entire suite of tools, improving both security and user experience at scale.

MyTaxi (Germany)

At MyTaxi (now FreeNow), I worked on a competitive project to Uber, focused on providing seamless ride-hailing services. This experience involved building scalable, efficient systems that could handle high volumes of transactions and dynamic user interactions in real time.

TCP-OCHIS (Malaysia)

While working at MIMOS, I contributed to TCP-OCHIS, an information system designed to manage and sync data across Malaysia's national clinics. One of the biggest challenges was ensuring real-time synchronization of information at both the local workstation level (within a clinic) and across clinics syncing to the cloud.

Openet (Malaysia)

Working in the R&D department of Openet, an Irish company in Malaysia, I developed machine learning algorithms for anomaly detection. This work involved analyzing complex data patterns to identify unusual behaviors and potential issues in telecommunications systems.

Newspaper (Iran)

I was part of the development team for one of Iran's largest newspapers, working on their internal systems and digital presence. This role gave me hands-on experience in high-traffic environments, complex content management, and large-scale data handling.
